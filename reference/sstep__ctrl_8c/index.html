    Sampling window: the legs may use the compare range 0 . 

    static void derive_window (
    struct sstep_ctx * m
)

    . P - W only, which acts like a bus voltage of Vbus (P - W) / P. inv_vbus is scaled by bus_num / bus_den (P / (P - W)), the bus voltage of the applied phase voltages by bus_frac. With W = 0 both ratios are 1 / 1 and nothing changes.
